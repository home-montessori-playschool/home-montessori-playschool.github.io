Why Burlingame Families Choose Us

The warmth of home, the intentionality of Montessori.

Unlike large institutional centers, Home Montessori Play-School moves at each child’s natural developmental pace—welcoming infants as young as 4 months through 4 years old and building confident early school readiness.

🌱

Welcoming Infants from 4 Months

We accept babies younger than most Montessori schools, honoring each infant’s personal bottle schedule, nap rhythm, and milestones—plus gentle, active potty-training support when ready.

🏡

Intimate 4–6 Child Community

Licensed for a maximum of 8 children (with typical daily groups of 4 to 6), Miss Leah provides deep personal attention while fostering close friendships, kindness, and peer respect.

🥣

Breakfast, Snacks & Park Outings

Wholesome breakfasts and snacks are provided daily, while parents pack their child’s preferred lunch from home. Children also enjoy water play, cooking classes, and outings to Trenton & Washington Park.

💬

Multilingual & Connected

English and Tagalog are formally taught (with Spanish also spoken). Parents receive regular updates, photos, and thoughtful developmental guidance throughout the week.

A Calm, Purposeful Flow

A Day at Home Montessori Play-School

Infants follow their own personalized feeding and sleep cues at any time, while toddlers and preschoolers thrive in a predictable, unhurried daily rhythm.

8:30 AM

Warm Arrival & Homemade Breakfast

Gentle greetings, health check-in, nutritious morning breakfast together, and settling into the classroom.

9:15 AM

Morning Montessori Work Cycle

Self-directed hands-on learning with wooden Montessori materials: practical life, language, reading, math, science & geography.

10:45 AM

Outdoor Play, Water Play & Park Trips

Fresh air in our outdoor space, sensory water play, gardening, or neighborhood walks to Trenton Park & Washington Park.

12:00 PM

Home-Packed Lunch & Restorative Nap Time

Children enjoy the preferred lunch packed from home by their parents, practicing mindful mealtime habits before cozy, individualized rest and nap schedules.

2:30 PM

Afternoon Snack, Art, Music & Yoga

Wholesome afternoon snack, creative art projects, music theory & appreciation, kids’ cooking, mindfulness, and yoga.

4:00 – 4:30 PM

Storytime, Tagalog & English Songs & Pickup

Group reflection, bilingual songs, tidy-up pride, and daily family updates with Miss Leah at pickup.
